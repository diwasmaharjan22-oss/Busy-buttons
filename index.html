Stop your child leaving this screen

  1. Open Settings, then Accessibility, then Guided Access. Turn it on and set a passcode there.
  2. Come back to this screen and press the side button three times quickly. On an older iPhone, press the Home button three times.
  3. Tap Start. Swiping and the side buttons now do nothing.
  4. To finish, press the side button three times again and enter your passcode.

This is a test version. Until you turn on Guided Access, your child can still swipe out of it.
